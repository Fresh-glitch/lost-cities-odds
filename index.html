How this was worked out, and what it does not cover

Exact, not sampled

Rolls within a pool are independent draws, so the chance a pool yields nothing is the average of (1 - q)^r over its roll count, and pools multiply. Nested table entries recurse. 593 loot tables were read in the game's own precedence order: the world save first, then mod jars, then the vanilla client jar.

Luck is zero

Every figure assumes no Luck. Bonus rolls and entry quality both scale with it, so a lucky player does a little better on the weighted tables.

Only what is in a file

A mod that injects into a vanilla table from Java at runtime is not counted here, because there is nothing on disk to read. That affects the shared vanilla sub-tables, not the seven Lost Cities tables, which are read exactly as the game wrote them into the world save.

Three dead entries in the pack

iceandfire:ice_dragon_cave, iceandfire:fire_dragon_cave and loottweaker:dragonsteel_gear are referenced by the basement, subway and rare tables but exist in no jar and in no script. Ice and Fire ships ice_dragon_female_cave and ice_dragon_male_cave, not the names used. Those entries are drawn and yield nothing, which is why basement and subway chests come up empty more often than their weights suggest.
